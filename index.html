About Us

A Sacramento-Based Studio
Focused on Real Emotion and Connection.

New Legacy Films is a Sacramento-based wedding videography studio, passionate about telling love stories through timeless imagery. We capture the emotion, beauty, and meaningful details of your day so you can relive every feeling for years to come.

Our approach is quiet and observant. We move through your day like guests who happen to carry a camera, catching the unguarded moments — the way your hands shake as you read your vows, the laughter that breaks through the tears. Every wedding film we create is crafted with care long after the last dance.

We move through your day like guests who happen to carry a camera, catching the unguarded moments — the quiet glances, joyful tears, and heartfelt laughter that make your wedding uniquely yours. Each wedding film is carefully crafted to preserve not only how your day looked, but how it felt.

Based in Sacramento, we proudly serve all of Northern California and are happy to travel anywhere in California — from the Bay Area and Los Angeles to wine country and San Diego — as well as destination weddings around the world.

Where We Go

Areas We Serve

We are proud Sacramento wedding videographers serving the greater Sacramento area — from the American River Parkway to the Delta, Folsom, El Dorado Hills, Granite Bay, and Roseville.

Love doesn't stay in one place — and neither do we. We travel anywhere throughout Northern California and all of California, including Napa & Sonoma wine country, the Bay Area, the Central Coast, Los Angeles, and San Diego.

For destination weddings beyond California — whether in Hawaii, New York, Italy, or anywhere your hearts lead — we are fully available. Destination travel fees may apply.
